# CREED Neurovascular Framework & Learning Academy

Designed by **Boge Expression by Babalola Oluwafemi Emmanuel** ([Link](https://oluwafemoli.github.io/boge-neurovascular-Framework-inteligence/)). High NFI built by **Boge-neurovascular-Framework-inteligence**.

## 🚀 Key Features & Architecture

1. **Structured & Clean Navigation Dropdowns**:
   - **Neuroscience**: Brain Anatomy, Neurobiology, Cognitive Neuroscience, Nervous System, Research Library.
   - **Examinations**: JAMB UTME CBT, WAEC SSCE, NECO Examination, GCE O/A Level, IELTS English, SAT & International, Official Syllabuses.
   - **Academy & Tools**: University Portals & Courses, Online Tutors & Lecturers, Assignment Submission, CREED AI Assistant & Flashcards, Scientific Calculator, Pomodoro Study Timer.
2. **120-Question Timed CBT Quiz Center**:
   - Covers **JAMB (20 Qs)**, **WAEC (20 Qs)**, **NECO (20 Qs)**, **GCE (20 Qs)**, **IELTS (20 Qs)**, **SAT (20 Qs)**, and **Neuroscience & STEM (20 Qs)** with selectable per-question countdown timers (45s, 60s, 90s), instant explanations, and live score tracking.
3. **Official Examination Syllabuses (User Dashboard)**:
   - Organized curriculum outlines for **JAMB, WAEC, NECO, and GCE** across **Science**, **Arts & Humanities**, **Commercial & Management**, and **International (IELTS/SAT/Neuroscience)** streams.
4. **Protected Study Tools & E-Learning**:
   - Scientific Calculator and Pomodoro Focus Timer gated inside the User Dashboard.
   - Student & Tutor roles with free tier (up to 2 online tutor connections) and paid monthly/lifetime options.
